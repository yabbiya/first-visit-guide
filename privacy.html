Privacy

Last updated: 2026-11-01

The short version

There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking cookies. We keep as little as possible.

Stored only in your browser

  • Your language, your switches (such as "With kids") and whether you've seen the quick guide.
  • If you bought the lifetime pass: your license key and a device ID, so the app stays unlocked on this device.

This data never leaves your device except when the app checks your license key (see below). Clearing your browser data removes it.

Visitor reports

If you post a one-line report (for example "Long line at the entrance"), the text is shown to everyone and deleted after 24 hours. Please don't include personal information. To stop spam, a one-way hash of your IP address is kept for 60 seconds; the IP address itself is not stored.

Payments and license keys

Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the merchant of record. We never see your card details. Lemon Squeezy shares your order details with us (such as your email address and the product you bought) so we can help with refunds and support. When you enter a license key, it is sent to Lemon Squeezy to confirm it is valid. See Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy.

Weather and crowd forecasts

To show the weather and crowd forecast, our server asks the Korea Meteorological Administration and the Korea Tourism Organization about the place you are viewing. Only the place's location is sent, nothing about you.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted on Vercel, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and operations. The font is loaded from the jsDelivr CDN, which sees your IP address when your browser downloads it.
