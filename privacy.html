Privacy

Last updated: 2026-11-14

The short version

There are no accounts, no ad banners and no tracking cookies on this site. We keep as little as possible. Some booking links are affiliate links (see below).

Stored only in your browser

  • Your language, your switches (such as "With kids") and whether you've seen the quick guide.

This data never leaves your device. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Visitor reports

If you post a one-line report (for example "Long line at the entrance"), the text is shown to everyone and deleted after 24 hours. Please don't include personal information. To stop spam, a one-way hash of your IP address is kept for 60 seconds; the IP address itself is not stored.

Payments

Payments for the Naver Map list are handled by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the merchant of record. We never see your card details. Lemon Squeezy shares your order details with us (such as your email address and the product you bought) so we can help with refunds and support. When you open the list, you are using Naver Map, and Naver's own privacy policy applies. See Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy.

Affiliate links (Klook)

Some booking links (for example hanbok rental, observatory tickets, the airport train, eSIMs and the WOWPASS card) go to Klook through an affiliate link. If you book through one, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. When you click, you leave this site; Klook may use cookies to record that you came from us, and your booking and payment are handled by Klook under its own privacy policy.

Weather and crowd forecasts

To show the weather and crowd forecast, our server asks the Korea Meteorological Administration and the Korea Tourism Organization about the place you are viewing. Only the place's location is sent, nothing about you.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted on Vercel, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and operations. The font is loaded from the jsDelivr CDN, which sees your IP address when your browser downloads it.
